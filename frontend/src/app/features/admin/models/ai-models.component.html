Candidate matching

Each job is turned into a checklist of must-haves and nice-to-haves. The AI checks every CV against it line by line (met, partial or missing) and quotes the evidence; the years of experience are checked by code. The score comes from the checklist (must-haves count double, a missing must-have caps the score), so every score can be verified by a recruiter.

The previous method (one overall AI score) was evaluated on a public data set of CV / job pairs labelled by recruiters and did not beat chance (ROC AUC 0.50 to 0.54). The ranking is a help to read CVs faster, not a decision.
